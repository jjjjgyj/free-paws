Free Paws · Neighbors helping pets and their people

A pet nearby needs a hand. So does their person.

Across New York, older and unwell neighbors struggle to walk the dogs they love, shelter animals wait for company, and rescues look for foster homes. Free Paws gathers their open requests in one place, refreshed every morning. Most ask for about an hour a week, and volunteering costs nothing.

Or offer to the people next door.

Most neighbors who need help never post a request. A short note in your building chat, on Nextdoor or in a local Buy Nothing group often reaches them. Make this one yours, then copy it.

Free Paws is an independent volunteer project. It is not run by or affiliated with the organizations listed; every request links to the group that posted it, and they decide who they work with. Know a group that should be here? Tell us on GitHub.
